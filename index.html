Philadelphia websites, apps & automation: FAQ

What does ClientForge do for Philadelphia businesses?

ClientForge builds websites, custom apps, AI automations and Meta ad campaigns for small and growing Philadelphia businesses. The goal is simple: more customers, and less busywork for the owner.

How much does a website cost?

The Complete Brand Setup Package (website up to 3 pages, logo, flyers, brand palette and social graphics) starts at $800. The Complete Business Package (3 to 5 page website, branding, CRM, automation and ads) starts at $1,200. Every price is a starting point; the final quote depends on the pages and features you need. See all pricing.

How fast can my website go live?

Most ClientForge websites and automations are delivered in under one week.

What can you automate for my business?

Lead follow-up by SMS and email, instant replies to new inquiries (speed to lead), booking and calendar flows, and CRM pipelines. Automation builds start at $250 one-time.

Do you build custom apps?

Yes. We build dashboards, client portals and internal tools around how your business already works, and you own what we build. Apps are quoted per project after a strategy call.

Do you run ads for local businesses?

Yes. Meta ads management starts at $250 per month (campaign setup, targeting, 2 creatives a month and a monthly report), with retargeting and weekly optimization as you scale. You pay Meta directly for ad spend.
